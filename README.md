## 🚀 About me

I’m a data and systems thinker with a background in financial-crime prevention and data analytics.

These days, I’m exploring where data, AI, knowledge and human systems meet - interconnection and communication. I enjoy finding the shape in messy information and complex systems, then turning it into clear, workable pieces. 

I’m fond of good questions, useful side quests and small experiments that grow into something worth sharing.

Most of my projects are currently incubating in private repositories. As I refine this profile and build my public presence, the ones ready to leave the playground will gradually emerge here. 


## 🌱🔭 Learning while building

I’m currently learning about LLMs, MCPs, Knowledge Graphs, and whatever sparks joy and interest while working on ways to understand, process and manage information — whether it is the way we communicate with AI (for example, context management or requirements management), or the way we connect and communicate with each other (social networks and language development).

Right now, that looks like:

- **AI, LLMs and agentic workflows** — exploring practical ways to work with AI: prompting, context, tool use, MCPs and small AI-assisted applications.
- **Knowledge graphs and Neo4j** — connecting my background in data modelling and financial-crime analysis with graph thinking, Cypher and relationship-based questions.
- **Personal knowledge and retrieval** — co-designing a Personal Knowledge Engine around a simple question: how can information be captured, found again and trusted when it matters?
- **Requirements and information design** — experimenting with visual requirements management, data dictionaries and clearer ways for people and AI systems to share context.
- **Python, data analysis and geospatial work** — building hands-on skills through data-science exercises, geospatial analysis and a volunteer project exploring crowd movement and amenity use at Roskilde Festival.
- **Social systems and communication** — learning social psychology, social computing, networks and language development, with curiosity about how people form, share and interpret information.
  - [Learning Danish](https://github.com/YellowDuck404/learning-danish)

Most of these are still local experiments, learning notes or early prototypes. More will appear here when it is ready to move from a local playground into a public space.
